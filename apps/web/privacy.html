Privacy Notice

TRANSPARENCY & DATA PROTECTION

1. Information We Collect

When you register and use the Deep Dive Learning Hub, we collect and store the following minimal data:

  • Email Address: Used strictly for account identification and authentication.
  • Password Hash: Salted, one-way cryptographic hash (bcrypt) used to verify logins. We never store plain-text passwords.
  • Tasks / Todos: The task items you create in the Todo workbench to sync them across your devices.
  • Tutorial Completion: Records indicating which guided tutorials you have marked as completed.
  • Preferences: Your selected interface theme (light or dark mode).

2. Purpose of Processing

Your data is processed solely to provide learning workbench state synchronization, authentication, and user isolation. We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with any third parties or advertisers.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained only for as long as your account exists. You may permanently delete your account at any time using the Delete account option in the user interface, which immediately and permanently purges your user record and all associated todos, progress records, and refresh tokens from our database.
